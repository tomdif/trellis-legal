Trellis

A mentor-mindset companion for parents, teachers, coaches and managers of young people: a folder per mentee, a morning brief with one tailored idea per person, and an in-the-moment coach.

Frequently asked

Where is my data stored? On your device only, and in your own iCloud or iTunes device backups. Trellis has no account and no server.

Does the coach send my notes anywhere? By default the coach runs on your device with Apple Intelligence (iOS 26 on supported iPhones). If you add your own Anthropic API key under You › Coach, folder summaries are sent to Anthropic only when you ask the coach; usage is billed to your key.

Why no ideas in the morning notification? The notification carries only the day's practice. Ideas about your mentees are generated when you open the app, so nothing about them appears on the lock screen.

Is this therapy? No. Trellis offers research-based coaching for everyday mentoring. If you are worried about a young person's safety, contact local emergency services or a crisis line.
