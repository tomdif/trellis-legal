Trellis privacy policy

Effective 15 October 2026. Trellis is published by Thomas Difiore.

What Trellis collects

Nothing. Trellis has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no server of its own. We never see your profile, your mentees' folders, your notes or your conversations.

Where your data lives

Everything you enter is stored on your device in the app's private database. It is included in your device backups (iCloud or computer) under Apple's backup protections. Deleting the app deletes the data on that device. "Start over" inside the app deletes everything.

The coach

On device. On iPhones that support Apple Intelligence (iOS 26 and later), the coach runs entirely on your device using Apple's on-device model. Nothing leaves your phone.

Cloud, optional. If you choose to add your own Anthropic API key under You › Coach, then when you ask the coach, generate a brief, or request a weekly look-back, the relevant folder summary and your question are sent to Anthropic's API using your key, and the reply is returned to your device. That data is handled under Anthropic's privacy policy and billed to your key. Your key is stored in the iOS Keychain and never sent anywhere except to Anthropic's API. Remove the key to stop all cloud use.

Notifications

With your permission, Trellis schedules a local morning notification on your device. It contains only the day's general practice, never anything about your mentees. No push service is involved.

Children

Trellis is for adults who mentor young people. It is not directed at children, and we collect no information from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. Questions: tomdif@gmail.com.
